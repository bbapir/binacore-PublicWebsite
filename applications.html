Applications

One engine, every kind of structure

OpenSees models far more than building frames. Binacore Desktop is the way in.

  • Buildings
  • Bridges
  • Special structures
  • Geotechnical and underground
  • Seismic isolation and damping

Buildings

Frames, walls and everything between them.

Multi-storey buildings

Concrete, steel and composite frames. Walls and cores, rigid floors, panel zones and plastic hinges.

Industrial buildings

Portal frames, crane buildings, pipe racks and platforms. Long spans and heavy point loads.

Masonry and infilled frames

Masonry walls and infilled frames. The strut that forms in the panel, and the frame after it cracks.

Bridges

From a single span to a cable-stayed deck.

Girder and arch bridges

Decks, piers and abutments on their bearings. Ground motion that reaches each support at its own time.

Cable-stayed and suspension bridges

Pylons, stays and main cables. Large displacements, followed step by step.

Special structures

Long spans, thin shells, tall towers.

Space structures

Space trusses and space frames, member by member.

Domes and shells

Ribbed domes, grid shells and curved roofs. Snap-through, traced past the limit point.

Tanks and silos

Thin shells under the pressure of what they hold, and the anchors that keep them down.

Towers and masts

Lattice towers, masts and chimneys. Slender structures under wind and earthquake.

Wind turbines

Tower, foundation and soil in one model, on land or on a monopile at sea.

Nuclear power plants

Containment and auxiliary buildings, with their soil, their isolation and their piping.

Geotechnical and underground

The structure ends at its foundation. The analysis does not have to.

Foundations and soil–structure interaction

Piles and footings on p-y, t-z and q-z springs, or the soil itself meshed around the foundation.

Site response analysis

A soil column from bedrock to surface. The motion that reaches the structure, layer by layer.

Liquefaction

Soil models that follow pore pressure, and what happens to the structure when the ground lets go.

Slopes and embankments

Embankments, earth dams and slopes, under gravity and shaking.

Tunnels and buried structures

Tunnels, culverts and basements, shaken together with the ground around them.

Seismic isolation and damping

The devices that take the earthquake, so the structure does not.

Base isolation

Elastomeric, lead-rubber and friction-pendulum bearings under the building, and the moat it moves in.

Dampers

Viscous and hysteretic dampers in the frame, each with its own law.

Buckling-restrained braces

Braces that yield in tension and in compression, cycle after cycle.

The same analyses, whatever the structure

All of them run in OpenSees. Every result is read on the model.

  • Static

    Load patterns, combinations, staged cases.

  • Modal

    Periods, shapes, mass participation.

  • Pushover

    Displacement-controlled, with the capacity curve.

  • Time history

    Ground motions, damping, the response at every step.

  • Response spectrum

    Mode by mode, then combined.

  • Multiple-support excitation

    A different motion at each support.
