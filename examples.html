Examples

Verified against reference models

The reference models of the OpenSeesPy documentation, rebuilt in Binacore Desktop and compared number by number.

  • Concrete frame under gravity

    A portal frame with fibre sections under its gravity load.

    Compared
    Vertical displacements of the beam–column joints
    Agreement
    Nine significant figures or better

  • Concrete frame, pushover

    The same frame pushed to a roof displacement of 15 in.

    Compared
    Base shear at five roof displacements
    Agreement
    Nine significant figures or better

  • Concrete frame under a ground motion

    The same frame through a nonlinear time history.

    Compared
    Roof displacement at its peak and at five instants
    Agreement
    Nine significant figures or better

  • Cantilever under a ground motion

    A single column with a mass at its tip.

    Compared
    Tip displacement at its peak and at five instants
    Agreement
    Nine significant figures or better

  • Moment–curvature of a concrete section

    A fibre section under axial load, bent step by step.

    Compared
    Moment at every one of 100 steps
    Agreement
    Nine significant figures or better

  • Response spectrum with rigid floors

    A 3D frame with rigid diaphragms.

    Compared
    The first five periods and the floor mode shapes
    Agreement
    Periods to nine figures or better, mode shapes to five

Where an example uses a recorded earthquake, both runs use the same substitute motion. The frame and the cantilever ship with the program, under File, Examples.
