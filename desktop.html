Built on OpenSees

OpenSees, the Open System for Earthquake Engineering Simulation, is developed at the University of California, Berkeley. It is the engine behind every analysis in Binacore Desktop.

Binacore Desktop is an independent interface to it, through OpenSeesPy. It is not an official OpenSees product and is not endorsed by its developers. OpenSees is not included: you install OpenSeesPy yourself, under its own licence.
